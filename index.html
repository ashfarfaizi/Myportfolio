Selected work

01

Low-Latency Matching Engine

Systems · C++20

A limit-order-book exchange core written from scratch in C++20: lock-free queues feed a matching thread pinned to its own core, with TCP order entry and gapless UDP market data. ~43 ns median order insert (6–7M ops/sec on one core), verified by a 200k-op differential fuzz test, ThreadSanitizer, and a journal + replay that reproduces 1.68M events to the same hash. The book is compiled to WebAssembly so you can trade against it in the browser.

  • C++20
  • Lock-free
  • TCP / UDP
  • WebAssembly
  • Linux

02

ReviewerGap — AI Oversight Study

AI Safety · Research

An empirical study of scalable oversight: when one model checks another's work, which errors does it catch, and do two reviewers miss the same ones? Problems are generated with their worked solutions, so the ground truth is known exactly — no human or model grading to trust. It plants one known error per solution, measures detection and localisation, and reports how correlated two reviewers' misses are: when that correlation is high, stacking reviewers buys less safety than it looks.

  • AI Safety
  • Scalable Oversight
  • Python
  • LLM Evaluation
